# SUS Digital Labs

**Open-source engineering for digital health and public-health data in Brazil.**

SUS Digital Labs is an independent open-source initiative focused on building and contributing to software for digital health, interoperability, public-health analytics and health-data engineering.

## What we work on
- Digital health and Primary Health Care
- SUS and e-SUS APS interoperability
- Public-health analytics
- Epidemiological intelligence
- Health data engineering
- Secure data synchronization
- Open standards and interoperability
- Developer tooling for public-sector health systems

## Projects

### gerencia-aps
Open-source analytics and synchronization tooling for Brazilian Primary Health Care data.
**Focus:** primary-health-care · health-analytics · typescript

## Open Source
We believe public-interest software benefits from transparent engineering, reproducible research, open standards and collaborative development.

## Engineering principles
- Security and privacy by design
- Interoperability first
- Reproducible engineering
- Clear APIs and data contracts
- Automated testing
- Observable systems
- Responsible handling of health data

## Governance
Technical decisions are made via public issues and Pull Requests. Significant changes require discussion and maintainer review. Security matters strictly follow our responsible disclosure policy.
